PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

Draft for owner review — last updated October 18, 2026

This page describes the intended privacy practices for Fuel the Fire, a school food-service inventory and meal-reporting application. The application owner must review and confirm these statements before public release. This draft is not a substitute for legal review.

Information the app may process

Depending on how an organization uses Fuel the Fire, the service may process account information such as email address and authentication identifiers, employee or school assignments, inventory records, stock movements, product and purchasing records, and meal or operational reports entered by authorized users.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users and provide the inventory, reporting, purchasing, and administrative functions enabled for their account. The app should not be used to enter student names, student identifiers, payment-card data, or other sensitive information unless the organization has specifically approved that use and the service has been assessed for it.

Service providers and storage

Fuel the Fire currently uses Supabase services for authentication and application data. Requests are sent over HTTPS. The owner must confirm the active project, its hosting and retention settings, access controls, backups, and any other providers before publication. The app may also rely on the device's browser or operating system for local storage and session handling.

Access and security

Access should be limited to authorized accounts and enforced through server-side permissions and database Row Level Security. Users should keep their sign-in credentials private, lock shared devices, and sign out when finished. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security; report suspected unauthorized access to the organization's administrator promptly.

Retention and deletion

Application records may remain stored while needed to operate the service or meet the organization's recordkeeping requirements. The owner must establish a retention schedule and a process for account access, correction, export, and deletion requests before launch. Contact the administrator for requests concerning your account or records.

Children's privacy

Fuel the Fire is intended for authorized school food-service staff and administrators, not for direct use by children. Do not enter identifiable student information into the application unless the organization has explicitly authorized that data processing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact your Fuel the Fire administrator. Before public release, the owner must replace this instruction with a monitored support email or other reliable contact method.

Publication checklist: confirm the statements above match actual app behaviour, add a real support contact, document retention/deletion procedures, verify Supabase access policies, and complete Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data safety disclosures.
