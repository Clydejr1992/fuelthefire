Fuel the Fire, wherever you work.

Use Fuel the Fire on your phone, tablet, or computer. You can add a shortcut to your device's home screen for quick access.

Sign in to Fuel the Fire

Install on your device

1

iPhone or iPad

Open Fuel the Fire in Safari, tap the Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

2

Android phone or tablet

Open Fuel the Fire in Chrome. Open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen, if offered.

3

Mac or Windows computer

Open Fuel the Fire in a supported browser. Look for the install icon in the address bar or use the browser menu's Install app option, if available.

About the app-store versions

Dedicated iPhone and Android store versions are being prepared. They are not available to download from the Apple App Store or Google Play yet. This page gives you instructions for adding the existing web app to your device.

For security, only sign in using the account provided by your Fuel the Fire administrator. Keep your device locked and sign out when using a shared device.
